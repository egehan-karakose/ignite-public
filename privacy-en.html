Information on your device

Ignite is a face-to-face game for two adults sharing one phone. You do not create an Ignite account. The app stores the display names you enter, pairing identifiers, card language, level progress, session counts, timestamps and identifiers of cards already shown in app storage on your device. It also stores your interface language and cached card content.

Your birth date is used to check whether you are at least 18. The date itself is not saved; a successful age check and its timestamp are stored. Spoken answers, physical activities and individual consent decisions are not recorded. There is no camera or microphone capture in the current app.

If local records cannot be read, the app preserves the original damaged data in a local recovery archive and retains readable pairings. The archive may include names and gameplay history. You see a notice when this happens.

Ignite does not automatically send pairing names, gameplay progress, seen-card history or recovery archives to its developer or RevenueCat. Device operating-system backup and transfer features may handle app storage according to your device settings; they are separate from an Ignite account or cloud-sync service.

Transfers you choose

You can display a pairing as a QR code or copy its transfer text. You can also explicitly copy a recovery archive in Settings. These actions expose that information to whoever you show or send it to and to the destination you choose. Purchase rights are not part of a pairing transfer. The app does not automatically email or upload these exports.

Purchases

Apple or Google handles payment. RevenueCat verifies purchases and maintains access to paid levels using purchase history and an automatically generated app user identifier. Purchase information also supports RevenueCat's purchase reporting. The current app does not provide RevenueCat with pairing names, your birth date, email address or a custom account ID. It does not contain an advertising attribution integration or gameplay analytics SDK.

We do not receive your payment-card details through Ignite. Removing local gameplay data does not delete Apple, Google or RevenueCat transaction records, revoke a purchase or request a refund.

Network requests and support

The app checks for updated card packs over HTTPS when content hosting is configured. It requests static content and does not attach pairing names or progress. The hosting service necessarily receives connection information such as your IP address when serving a request. The card files and these pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. GitHub controls the retention of its infrastructure logs under its privacy statement; we do not set that retention period. These pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts, embedded third-party content or contact forms.

Opening support or privacy links opens a website outside the app. If you contact support, the information you choose to provide and your return address will be used to handle your request. Support email is handled through Gmail (Google). Correspondence is used to respond, investigate the issue and handle related requests. We keep correspondence for as long as needed for those purposes and any applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion at the contact address below. Do not send passwords, private keys, payment-card numbers or private gameplay exports unless you deliberately choose to provide information needed for support.

Your choices and deletion

Delete an individual pairing from its details or choose Settings → Delete gameplay data to remove pairings, progress, age-check records and local recovery archives. Interface preferences, downloaded content and purchase-service records are separate. A copy previously exported or included in an operating-system backup may remain outside the app's current storage.

For questions or requests about support or purchase-service data, contact egehankarakose@gmail.com. You may request access, correction or deletion of data we process. To locate a purchase-service record and verify a request, we may ask for the store transaction identifier and information about the affected purchase. We do not ask for your store password or payment-card number. We review the request and any legal retention requirements; store and service providers may retain records they are required to keep. Because there is no Ignite account, an email address alone may not identify an anonymous purchase record. A data-deletion request is separate from a store refund request.

Audience and changes

Ignite is intended for adults aged 18 and over. Later levels contain romantic/intimate questions and physical-touch activities. We will update this page when the app's data practices change.
